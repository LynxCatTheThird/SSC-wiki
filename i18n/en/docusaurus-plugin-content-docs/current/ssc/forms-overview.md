# SSC form overview

SSC currently provides seven normal form lines and two special forms:

| Form line | Stages | Main play pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bat | `bat_0`–`bat_3` | Night movement, glide, and attachment |
| Axolotl | `axolotl_0`–`axolotl_3` | Water, wetness, regeneration, and water dash |
| Ocelot | `ocelot_0`–`ocelot_3` | Stealth, climbing, pounce, and claw combat |
| Familiar Fox | `familiar_fox_0`–`familiar_fox_3` | Witch affinity, mana, potions, and fire magic |
| Snow Fox | `snow_fox_0`–`snow_fox_3` | Jumps, landing attacks, snowballs, and cold |
| Anubis Wolf | `anubis_wolf_0`–`anubis_wolf_3` | Wither, undead traits, wolves, and death effects |
| Spider | `spider_0`–`spider_3` | Webs, climbing, cocoons, and bridges |
| Allay special | `allay_sp` | Flight and ranged support |
| Feral Cat special | `feral_cat_sp` | Four-legged stealth hunting |

Open a form page for stage restrictions and recovery instructions. SSCA endgame branches start from selected stage-4 forms.
