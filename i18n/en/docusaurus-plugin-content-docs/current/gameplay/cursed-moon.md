# Cursed Moon

The Cursed Moon is a world event that changes the rules of several forms. It is detected from the moon phase and night state, not from a command or a visual overlay alone. During the event, forms with moon-related abilities can gain shorter cooldowns, stronger resource recovery, or access to a temporary branch.

Check the current form page before relying on a bonus. A moon bonus never bypasses a required stage, item, or evolution condition. SSCA routes that require a Cursed Moon night are marked in the evolution diagram.
