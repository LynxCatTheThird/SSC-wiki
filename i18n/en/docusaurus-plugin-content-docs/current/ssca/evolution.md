# Evolution system

SSCA has two entry patterns. Evolved Familiar Fox and Evolved Axolotl begin from the starter book, earn upgrade points through experience, and unlock their end forms at level 50. Existing SSC stage-4 forms branch with a Moon Marrow Cross or an evolution stone.

The Moon Marrow Cross normally requires a Cursed Moon night and selects the moon branch. The evolution stone selects the alternate branch. Special forms cannot be reset with an ordinary inhibitor; a failed attempt follows the result listed on the route page.
